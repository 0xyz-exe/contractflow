# ContractFlow User Guide

## Getting Started

1. **Log in:**
   - Use one of the pre-configured accounts:
     - Admin / Executive: `admin@contractflow.org` / `AdminPass123!`
     - Legal Counsel: `legal@contractflow.org` / `LegalPass123!`
     - Department Manager: `manager@contractflow.org` / `ManagerPass123!`
     - Finance: `finance@contractflow.org` / `FinancePass123!`

2. **Drafting a Contract:**
   - Click "Draft New Contract" from the sidebar or dashboard.
   - Choose a pre-approved template (e.g., NDA, MSA, SaaS, SOW) to prepopulate clauses.
   - Input Counterparty entity name, estimated spend value, and department.
   - Click "Save & Generate Draft Reference".

3. **Submitting for Approval:**
   - Inspect the generated draft.
   - Click "Submit to Approval Workflow".
   - The multi-tier approval engine assigns sequential tasks to Department Managers, Legal Counsel, Finance, and Executive Leadership (for contracts > $50,000).

4. **Reviewing and Approving:**
   - Navigate to "My Approvals".
   - Inspect the contract details, document version, and comments.
   - Select "Approve Stage", "Reject", or "Request Revisions" and provide mandatory justification.

5. **Tracking Obligations:**
   - Check the "Obligations & Deadlines" tab for SOC2 audits, payment milestones, and insurance certificate renewals.
   - Click "Mark Fulfilled" when compliance evidence is verified.
